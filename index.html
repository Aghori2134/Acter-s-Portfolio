About Me

I am an actor and director from Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Maharashtra. I graduated in theatre from Lalit Kala Kendra, Savitribai Phule Pune University, and have 7 years of theatre experience. I later worked as a lead actor in an art film at SRFTI, Kolkata, and spent 6 years in the Marathi television industry as an actor and associate director. I currently work with a social media agency as an actor, anchor and content creator. I also have experience living and working in Pune and Mumbai for shoots and professional projects.
